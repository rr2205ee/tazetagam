About the enterprise

An integrated farm, growing since 2014

TAZE TAGAM produces both table eggs and poultry meat at scale in Ahal Velayat, Turkmenistan — and brings them to local and international markets.

2014
Operating and growing since
Our story

From two farms to export markets

Since 2014 we have been operating and steadily growing as a dual-stream agricultural enterprise — combining high-volume egg production with advanced broiler rearing for premium poultry meat.

We operate under the Garagum Farmers' Association in Ak Bugday Etrap, Ahal Velayat, and are a member of the Union of Entrepreneurs and Industrialists of Turkmenistan. Our goal is simple: dependable, fresh products at fair prices, produced to a consistent standard across both of our farms.

200,000Egg-laying hens in modern, climate-controlled houses.
200,000Broilers reared simultaneously for premium poultry-meat processing.
Milestones

Our journey so far

  1. 2014

    Operations begin

    We start operating in Ahal Velayat as a dual-stream poultry enterprise: table eggs and poultry meat.

  2. March 2025

    17th Anniversary Award

    Diploma from the Union of Entrepreneurs and Industrialists of Turkmenistan, at the exhibition marking its 17th anniversary.

  3. July 2025

    Export Achievement, Istanbul

    Recognised at the Turkmenistan Export Goods Fair in Istanbul, together with the Chamber of Commerce.

  4. Today

    Two farms, three export markets

    200,000 laying hens and 200,000 broilers, supplying local buyers and markets in Dubai, Qatar and Afghanistan.
